Words

Teach Murmur the names and jargon you use, and phrases that type longer text for you.

Words
Names, products and jargon. Murmur listens for them and writes them exactly like this. If it keeps writing one wrong, add what it writes as "heard as". Case doesn't matter in what it hears.
Learn from my fixes
Correct a word Murmur got wrong and it's added here, marked Learned. Undo it from the bar.
Snippets
Say a phrase, get your text: an address, a sign-off, a link. Phrases like "my email" work mid-sentence too; a single word like "email" only works said on its own.

Settings

Murmur types wherever your cursor is. Here's how you start it, and what it does with your words.

Shortcut
Push to talk
Hold it while you speak and let go to type. Pick a key you don't use for typing.
Press your new shortcut
Hands-free
Double-tap the shortcut, or click the bar at the bottom of your screen. Tap the shortcut again or click stop to finish.
Right Alt, F9 and Ctrl + Shift + Space all work well.
General
Start with Windows
Murmur starts quietly in the tray when you sign in, ready when you are.
Microphone
System default follows your Windows sound settings.
Sounds
A soft chime when Murmur starts and stops listening.
Save memory when idle
Frees about 700 MB after 10 minutes without dictating. You can still start speaking at once; that first text takes a few seconds longer.
Text
Remove filler words
Leaves out "um", "uh", "hmm" and stutters like "to to".
Write numbers as digits
"Twenty five dollars" becomes $25, "three thirty pm" becomes 3:30 PM.
Voice commands
Say "new line" or "new paragraph" to start one.
Appearance
Theme
System follows your Windows setting.
Show the bar when idle
The small bar at the bottom of your screen. Off, or over a fullscreen video or game, it only appears while you dictate.
History
Keep history
How long your dictations stay on this PC. Off stops saving new ones.
Export
Save everything you've dictated as a Markdown or text file.
Clear history
Deletes every dictation saved on this PC. This can't be undone.
Privacy
Your voice never leaves this PC
Speech is turned into text on your own computer. There is no account, and nothing is sent over the internet.
